# Evidence Register And Approval States

This document explains how checklist workflows should connect evidence and approval logic.

## Typical States

Common workflow states include:

- draft
- in review
- changes requested
- approved
- final

## Core Roles

Checklist workflows typically include:

- owner
- reviewer
- approver

## Why It Matters

A checklist becomes operational when it is connected to evidence quality and role-based decisions. Without this linkage, checklist completion can look complete even though critical evidence is missing or not approved.
